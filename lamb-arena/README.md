# MBËR ARENA — Modern Lamb Training Center

Standalone concept site for a new business project: a modern indoor training
center for traditional Senegalese wrestling (lamb / laamb).

**This project is independent from the coaching website at the repository root.**

## The concept

- Indoor gym (walled open space or large hall) dedicated to lamb
- **4 square sand rings** in a 2×2 grid, for kids and adults
- **Digital LED advertising boards** around the perimeter, like the digital
  billboards seen in televised football matches — sold to sponsors in rotations
- **Vending machine corner** (drinks, biscuits, snacks) as passive revenue
- Changing rooms, showers, reception

## What's in this folder

- `index.html` — self-contained one-page concept site (French), dark cinematic
  "Higgsfield-style" design. Includes:
  - An SVG floor plan of the arena (4 sand rings, LED perimeter, vending corner)
  - Programs & pricing for kids, adults and competitors
  - Sponsor/LED business model section
  - **6 ready-to-copy Higgsfield AI prompts** to generate photorealistic
    concept images of the arena (overall view, ring action, kids class,
    vending corner, building facade, event night)

## Preview locally

Open `lamb-arena/index.html` in any browser — no build step, no dependencies
(only Google Fonts loaded from CDN).
